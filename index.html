About SKSBV

Shaping Tomorrow with Faith & Character

The Samastha Kerala Sunni Bala Vedi (SKSBV) is the apex student organization under the aegis of the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. Established in 1993, SKSBV steers millions of young minds toward moral uprightness and intellectual capability.

Guided by the Jem-Iyyathul Mu'allimeen Central Council, we safeguard students from moral disorientation while cultivating deep, respectful teacher-student camaraderie.

Key Initiatives

Our Core Activities

Empowering students across Kerala through comprehensive programs and support networks.

Educational Support & Training

Empowering madrasa students with academic scholarships, skill development, moral camps, and guidance for holistic worldly and spiritual success.

Arts, Culture & Literature

Nurturing creative talents through state-level Sahithyotsavs, literary societies, speech competitions, and library initiatives.

Community Outreach & Social Care
