About SKSBV

Shaping Tomorrow with Faith & Character

The Samastha Kerala Sunni Bala Vedi (SKSBV) is the apex student organization under the aegis of the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. Established in 1993, SKSBV steers millions of young minds toward moral uprightness and intellectual capability.

Guided by the Jem-Iyyathul Mu'allimeen Central Council, we safeguard students from moral disorientation while cultivating deep, respectful teacher-student camaraderie.

Our Mission

Moral & Spiritual Foundation
