ABOUT SKSBV

Shaping the Future with
Faith & Discipline

The Samastha Kerala Sunni Bala Vedi (SKSBV) is a premier student platform under the Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board. Formed in 1993, we are dedicated to nurturing the next generation with religious knowledge and moral discipline.

Controlled by the Mu'allimeen Central Council, we strive to safeguard students from deviant ideologies while fostering a deep-rooted bond between mentors and students.

Mission

Based on the principles of Samastha, fostering discipline, good conduct, and strong teacher-student bonds.

Vision

Instilling religious thoughts and informing the new generation about our rich history and core values.

Values

Upholding Ahlussunnah wal Jama'ah principles and honoring mentors with utmost discipline.

Our Core Values

Upholding the traditions of Samastha with a vision for the future

Islamic Foundation

Instilling the theological principles of Ahlussunnah wal Jama'ah to ensure students are grounded in traditional Sunni Islam.

Moral Discipline

Fostering a sense of discipline and good conduct, creating a generation with strong moral and cultural values.

Teacher-Student Bond

Strengthening the relationship between students and teachers, emphasizing respect and honoring mentors.

Guarding Faith

Cautioning students about the dangers of atheistic ideologies and deviant acts to protect their faith.
